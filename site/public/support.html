Put it on your iPhone

  1. Open the game in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Deck of Fools from your Home Screen and turn your phone sideways. It plays full screen, like an app, and keeps working offline once loaded.

Keeping your progress

You start as a guest, and your game is saved on your device and on our server. To play on another device without losing anything, open Profile and create an account with your email, then sign in with it on the other device.

Common questions

  • The text is too small or too big. Open Settings and change Text & cards.
  • I want the old scanline look. Settings → CRT effect.
  • How do I delete my account? Profile → Delete account, then tap again to confirm. It removes your account and all its data from our server. See the Privacy Policy.
  • I forgot my password. On the sign-in sheet, tap “Forgot password?” and follow the email.
